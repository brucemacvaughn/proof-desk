# About the delay in the March shipment

I want to explain what happened with the March order, because the email from our
logistics partner was not clear and I think you deserve better explanation.

The container left Ningbo on 3 March as planned. It arrived Rotterdam on 28
March, which is four days late, but this was not the main problem. The problem
was customs. Our HS code for the aluminium brackets was wrong — we used 7616.99
and the correct one is 7610.90. This is my mistake, I approved the paperwork.

The correction took eleven working days. In this time the container sat in the
port and we paid demurrage of 340 euro per day. Total additional cost is 3,740
euro, and we will absorb this, not pass to you.

For the next shipment I have asked our broker to check codes before we submit,
not after. Also I will send you the paperwork two days before departure so you
can look if you want. I am sorry for the delay and for the poor communication
from our side.
